# Product Strategy

## Positioning

SignalKit is the **opportunity-to-plan layer** for product builders. Competing "AI builder" tools jump straight from prompt to code and produce throwaway apps with no evidence trail. SignalKit instead produces a **trustworthy, evidence-backed, build-ready plan** that any team or AI coding agent can execute. The plan — not an app — is the product.

## Why this wins

1. **Trust as a feature.** Every conclusion is linked to evidence, confidence, assumptions and contradictions. This is what makes the output usable for B2B, investors and agencies.
2. **Global & multilingual from day one.** Opportunities and documents are produced for specific markets and languages, not English-only.
3. **BYOK LLM marketplace.** Users pick their own models with full price/context/quality transparency, like Roo Code. No lock-in to one provider.
4. **Role-aware exports.** One pack yields founder, PM, designer, engineering, growth, investor and AI-agent deliverables.
5. **Compounding moat.** Outcome tracking (which niches/markets/models actually led to launches) feeds back into scoring and recommendations (Session 20).

## Monetization

Tiered SaaS plans (Free, Founder Pro, Agency, Studio, Enterprise) gating workspaces, seats, niche searches, packs, exports, AI budget, white-label and API access. Credits meter heavy generation. BYOK can bypass platform LLM cost while still counting platform usage. (Session 15.)

## Sequencing

The platform is built as 20 sequential sessions. The first 10 establish the full core (foundation, DB/auth/RBAC, UI/i18n, LLM marketplace, LLM router, geo, ingestion, evidence, scoring, Product Pack). The second 10 extend it (governance, exports, deploy, mobile, billing, collaboration, white-label, API, quality intelligence, growth loops). See [BUILD_LOG.md](BUILD_LOG.md).

## Non-negotiable principle

The system is built with full architecture from day one — no temporary layers, no duplicated systems for language/geo/LLM/evidence/documents, and no UI redesign later because the design law exists from Session 3.
